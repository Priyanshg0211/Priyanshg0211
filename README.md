## About

I'm the founder of **PeerX**, a platform that helps college students discover the internships, hackathons, workshops, tech fests, and online events worth their time — curated in one place instead of scattered across a dozen websites and group chats.

I build the product end to end: engineering it in Flutter and designing it in Figma.

- PeerX is live on the Play Store, helping students find and act on opportunities faster
- Featured on **Shark Tank India**
- Semifinalist, **Buildathon 2.0** by 100x Engineers

## Building PeerX

**Engineering** — Flutter, with hands-on depth in:
- Navigation and state management (Bloc / GetX)
- Testing and deployment pipelines
- Firebase for auth, Firestore, and backend infrastructure

**Design** — every feature starts as a wireframe before it becomes a line of code:
- Figma and Adobe XD for design and prototyping
- Google UX Design certifications — UX process, wireframing, prototyping

That combination is the point: PeerX is shaped by how students actually search for opportunities, not just what's easy to build.

## Stack

Flutter · Dart · Firebase · Figma · Adobe XD · Postman · Git

Scaling PeerX to more campuses, iterating from real student feedback, and continuing to build at the intersection of design and development.
